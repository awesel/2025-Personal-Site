Andrew Wesel

Member of Technical Staff at Simile

I am curious about decision-making: why do people make the decisions that they do, can we train models that mimic and understand human behavior, and can we build systems that help people and organizations make good decisions?

At Simile, I work on training, evaluating, and serving our language models. There are a variety of problems that these models solve: we simulate human-like speech from a diverse set of plausible viewpoints, we predict what the distribution of behaviors will be for groups of people in arbitrary scenarios, and we orchestrate large amounts of these individual simulations and predictions into an observable parallel universe. I am very grateful to be mentored by Percy Liang.

In my spare time, I am also trying to finish my undergrad degree at Stanford.
