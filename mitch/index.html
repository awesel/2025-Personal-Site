How does this work?
  1. Daily, there will be a question.
  2. Answer it.
  3. Guess the distribution that will result from other players of the game answering the question.
  4. Your score is high if your guess is close, and low if it is far.
  5. The distribution will not be revealed until the end of the day (midnight PT), but you may see your position in the leaderboard. Of course, with sufficient guesses, you may estimate the true distribution, but please abide by the honor code and do not do this.
